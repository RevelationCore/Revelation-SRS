## Reviewer instruction

Review sector accuracy and operational feasibility independently of current Revelation implementation. A product gap is recorded as a gap; it is not a reason to rewrite sector practice around existing code.

For every assigned range:

1. Confirm the boundary, accountable owner and system of record.
2. Reproduce the main flow from the cited evidence.
3. Confirm alternatives and exceptions have an owner and terminal/rejoin outcome.
4. Validate data minimisation, effective dating, correction and retention.
5. Validate system acknowledgement, retry and reconciliation.
6. Review the relevant national section and provider-policy configuration.
7. Decide each open question.
8. Record `Approved`, `Approved with actions`, `Return for amendment`, or `Rejected`.

## Four-nation prompts

### England

- Is an OfS requirement incorrectly presented as universal?
- Are OIA references correctly limited to its jurisdiction?
- Do alternative-provider or FE-delivered HE responsibilities need a variant?

### Scotland

- Are SCQF, matriculation/course-enrolment and ordinary/honours terms correct?
- Are SFC and SAAS responsibilities separated from HESA/Jisc and SLC?
- Does a page assume an England-style programme or assessment structure?

### Wales

- Are Medr, Student Finance Wales, CQFW and Welsh-medium requirements represented?
- Are awarding-provider and collaborative-partner responsibilities explicit?
- Is bilingual operation represented as a service/data requirement?

### Northern Ireland

- Are Department for the Economy and Student Finance NI responsibilities explicit?
- Are OIA or Great Britain equality-law statements incorrectly applied?
- Are cross-border, partner and provider-regulation variants represented?
