## Source quality notes

- Provider pages demonstrate institutional practice; they do not establish a national mandate.
- UK-wide student finance administration is delivered through related national schemes. A common SLC hand-off must not erase scheme, domicile, course, or mode differences.
- “Registration”, “enrolment”, “re-enrolment”, “re-registration” and “matriculation” are not used consistently across institutions. Process pages use canonical outcome language and record local terms as variants.
- Immigration guidance changes frequently. Pages relying on SRC-001 or SRC-002 require a currency check before approval or implementation.
