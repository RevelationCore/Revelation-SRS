## Summary

The conferment chain is well designed. The recommender cannot confer, conferment refuses if the calculation has changed since the recommendation, and the award is append-only. Signed documents with account-free verification are a strong base for D-20 and D-21. The gaps are in what happens around the calculation and after conferment. Missing results produce a resit or repeat-year decision rather than a deferral. There is no route for the board's discretion on progression. D-12's attempt limit is not enforced. An award cannot be revoked or corrected. The certificate and HEAR omit facts a UK certificate or HEAR must state. The PGR research award skips the separation of duties used for taught awards. All six pages are still v0.1.

**Findings:** Blocker 0 · Major 11 · Minor 7 · Observation 2.

**Variants.** Classification schemes (Scottish honours versus ordinary degrees, integrated masters, NI and Welsh provider ordinances), credit-framework levels and exit awards are configuration through the D-35 rule types. The documents need code: bilingual Welsh and English certificates and HEARs (A2.1; Welsh Language Standards for Welsh providers) and joint or dual awards with more than one awarding body (BP-06-003 A2.1). The model has one awarding body per award. Graduation cycles are configuration. PGR ceremonies are a separate cycle, which the model allows.

**Data protection.** Public verification is consent-led and minimal (D-21), apart from G11. Graduation stores only a boolean for access needs, which is right for minimisation. HEAR section 6 will bring in extracurricular data: set the lawful basis (public task or legitimate interests) and the privacy-notice wording before it is built. Verification logs need a retention period (G11).
