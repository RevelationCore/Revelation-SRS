## Summary

The assessment chain from ADR-020 is mostly built and much stronger than the July process pages say. Pack hashing, decisions that cite the hash, rule-version evidence, and before and after versions on corrections are all real. PGR examination is built end to end. But several controls that the pages and the ADR treat as mandatory are not enforced. Quorum is not checked. Declared conflicts do not block a decision. The person who ratifies can also record the external examiner's sign-off. Moderation records evidence but does not gate anything. Students can read any module result in the tenant, including provisional ones. All eleven pages are still v0.1 and describe gaps that have since been closed.

**Findings:** Blocker 1 · Major 13 · Minor 8 · Observation 3.

**Variants.** The national variants (England OfS B4/B5; Scotland SCQF and the Quality Code for Scotland; Wales CQFW and Welsh-language documents; Northern Ireland DfE) are configuration, not code, for this domain: classification schemes, credit levels, terminology, and the "referral" or "resit" labels. One exception needs code: Welsh-language output for student-facing results and appeal text (BP-05-009 step 4), where the Welsh Language Standards apply to Welsh providers. There are no module-level controls for partner or collaborative provision (role-limited views, A2.1 in BP-05-007).

**Data protection.** F1 and F5 are the material issues. Misconduct and EC are correctly kept to outcomes under ADR-017 and D-5. The PGR examiner view correctly withholds the thesis until independence is checked. Board packs include EC and misconduct indicators: confirm with the DPO that pack access (`exam-board:read`, which includes `external-examiner`) is limited to what each role needs.
