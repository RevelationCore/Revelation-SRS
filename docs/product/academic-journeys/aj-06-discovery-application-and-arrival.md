# AJ-06 — Sam: from university discovery to arrival and first teaching

Status: **Proposed narrative v0.6**, 13 November 2026 · [Document status](README.md#status-of-these-documents).

[Shared scenario and course definition](README.md) · [Narrative catalogue](../academic-journey-narrative-catalogue.md) · [Preceding course-approval story](aj-01-course-creation-and-publication.md)

**Perspective:** Sam, a school-leaver deciding whether Northbridge is the right university, supported by recruitment, admissions, Registry and campus services. **Goal:** make an informed choice, secure a place and start the promised course with the necessary support and access. **Starts:** searching for a university. **Ends:** an authorised registration, reconciled core entitlements, arrival/induction and first teaching. Detailed registration, accommodation, module allocation and teaching processes belong to AJ-16/17/18/20; this story follows their outcomes from the student's perspective.

**Timeline:** t is Monday 23 September 2030, the start of academic year 2030/31; offsets are calendar weeks. See the [timeline convention](README.md#timeline-convention) and the [combined weekly timeline](README.md#combined-weekly-timeline) for dates.

## The story

### 1. Sam compares possible futures — t-52 to t-48

In autumn 2029, Sam is studying Mathematics, Geography and Computer Science and wants to use data to understand environmental problems. Sam compares several universities, looking beyond the course title: what is compulsory, which options are available, where teaching takes place, how work is assessed, what entry grades are needed and what attending would cost.

Northbridge's public prospectus shows Environmental Data Science for entry in 2030. Sam can read the content without an applicant account. The page identifies the entry year, full-time main-campus route and the approved specification `PROS-EDS-2030@1.0`. The academic course and diet behind it are the versions approved in AJ-01.

Sam saves the course and asks whether “field study” means everyone takes the second-year Community Field Study module. A recruitment adviser checks the approved specification: first-year Field and Laboratory Methods is compulsory; second-year Community Field Study is an option. The response explains the distinction and the option's availability conditions. It does not promise a seat. The adviser records the question, answer and public version used so another colleague can understand the conversation.

### 2. A visit makes the choice more tangible — t-47

Sam books an open day and asks for a step-free tour. The booking confirmation lists the session, meeting point, travel information and an accessible way to change the booking or contact the team. Event staff see the practical arrangements they need; academic admissions staff do not receive a diagnostic inference or use the request to assess suitability.

During the visit, Sam attends Aisha's subject talk, sees a computing lab, meets current students and views accommodation. Aisha describes the approved course and its intended first cohort. She distinguishes planned learning opportunities from confirmed individual module allocations. She cannot make an offer at the open day.

After the visit, Sam receives the requested follow-up information. The visit is recorded against the enquiry, not as university attendance or enrolment. Sam can stop optional marketing messages without losing necessary application communications. Sam is 17 at the visit. A parent accompanies Sam, but a guest is not automatically authorised to see an application or speak on Sam's behalf, and Northbridge's contact rules for under-18 visitors apply to the day itself.

### 3. The application becomes an institutional case — t-36 to t-33

In the fictional early-2030 application window, Sam applies through UCAS for Northbridge's Environmental Data Science course. The course and entry-year identifiers are checked against the approved listing. Sam's submitted qualifications, predictions, reference and other required application material reach the institutional admissions boundary through the agreed channel.

The intake records the external application reference and source version. It identifies the related enquiry using permitted evidence. A similar name or matching email alone would not authorise a destructive merge. Sam can see that the application has been received and what, if anything, is needed next; the system does not call Sam an enrolled student.

Admissions owns the assessment. The adviser checks completeness and the course-specific criteria under `ADMISSIONS-2030@1`. If a qualification is unclear, the case asks for specific evidence and displays an owner and deadline. On the main path the material is sufficient. Visit participation, marketing preferences and the step-free request are not selection criteria.

Admissions also assesses Sam's fee status under `FEES-2030@1`, separately from the academic decision. On the evidence in the application, Sam is assessed as home fee status. Had the evidence been unclear, Admissions would ask Sam for specific information before the offer, rather than issue an offer on a guessed fee basis. Fee status is not a selection criterion.

### 4. An offer states exactly what must happen — t-32 to t-24

An authorised admissions officer makes conditional offer `OFF-SAM-001@1` for `NBU-EDS@1.0`, route `EDS-FT@1.0`, entry cohort 2030. The offer states three A levels at grade B or better, including Mathematics at B or better. It states Sam's home fee status and references the applicable course information, fee policy and terms. Registration requirements are shown as later tasks rather than hidden extra academic offer conditions.

The decision is sent through the approved admissions/UCAS process and its acceptance is reconciled. Sam sees the official offer and selects Northbridge as the firm choice through the applicable UCAS route. The resulting response is recorded against the exact offer version. Northbridge's staff view and applicant view agree about the conditional offer and response.

Sam has chosen a preferred destination, but has not yet met the conditions or registered. The portal says so plainly. An offer response arriving twice does not create another application, place or account. A conflicting or stale response goes to an owned reconciliation task rather than silently replacing the current choice.

### 5. Waiting has useful, proportionate next steps — t-23 to t-7

Over the following months, Sam receives preparation information, can review the published course and can ask about accommodation and funding. Any room reservation has its own eligibility, cancellation and payment conditions; it is not evidence of a confirmed academic place. Where an enquiry must be referred to a specialist team, Sam can see who will respond.

The university prepares registration tasks but does not activate full student entitlements early. If Sam wants longer-term study adjustments, the support service explains the separate request and assessment route; the open-day access request is not repurposed as a permanent support decision.

If the university discovers a material contradiction between public course information and the offer, the adviser must resolve it with the academic, admissions and publication owners. Sam would receive an explanation and any required choices against retained versions. Neither a refreshed web page nor an administrator editing the offer in place is an adequate resolution. That is an alternative branch, not an unexplained change in this main path.

### 6. Results are checked, not assumed — t-6 to t-5

In summer 2030, Sam obtains Mathematics A, Geography B and Computer Science B. Sam sees these results before the university has completed all its checks. The initial authorised results import contains Mathematics and Computer Science but is missing Geography. The application displays “Awaiting verification of Geography result”, with an admissions owner; it does not label Sam unsuccessful or confirmed.

The admissions team reconciles the missing item with the permitted results source. A later verified source version supplies Geography B. The condition record now shows the evidence for all three grades and the Mathematics subject requirement. A self-reported screenshot can support an enquiry, but it does not replace the authorised evidence in this fixture.

The authorised officer confirms that the offer conditions are met and records the place decision. The institutional and UCAS states are reconciled through the supported process. Sam receives the confirmed-place communication only when the relevant decision and channel state support it. A duplicate results file cannot create a second confirmation, while a corrected later result must trigger impact review rather than overwrite the original silently.

### 7. A place becomes a registration journey — t-4 to t-2

The admissions outcome passes to Registry with its application, offer and evidence references. Registry creates or reuses the canonical person and prospective-student record and prepares registration. Sam receives an applicant/registration account with the access needed at this stage; full student permissions are still separate.

Sam verifies personal details, supplies the required identity evidence, reviews the relevant terms and completes the appropriate fee/funding tasks. Academic and financial registration checks have their own visible states and decision owners. If an invitation or verification service fails, the team can recover the task without creating another person or treating a delivery failure as an admissions rejection.

On the main path the checks complete, Registry authorises enrolment `ENR-SAM-2030-01`, and the enrolment is bound to `NBU-EDS@1.0`, `EDS-FT@1.0` and `EDS-2030@1.0`. Fee liability and any applicable student-finance confirmation follow the configured eligible route. No funding eligibility is inferred from having applied through UCAS.

### 8. The promised experience reaches real services — t-2 to t-1

Sam's student account is provisioned, the library recognises the relevant entitlement, and the portal shows the agreed first-stage study. The six compulsory first-stage modules are registered through the authorised module-registration process; a curriculum definition alone would not have created them. Those registrations produce the VLE and teaching/attendance rosters.

Sam can distinguish a pending timetable from a final one. If a service has not acknowledged the right registration or access version, the portal gives a useful status and a support route. Registry and service owners reconcile those hand-offs; an email saying “welcome” is not their evidence that the VLE actually works.

Accommodation confirms Sam's allocation and check-in arrangements under its own process. The university sends only the student context needed for that service. The room booking does not control Sam's academic status.

### 9. Arrival is the start of study, not a shortcut around registration — t to t+1

At t, the September 2030 academic year begins and Sam arrives, checks into accommodation, attends induction and meets the personal tutor. Staff can see the relevant registration and service status without searching private support evidence. An induction check-in records the event; it cannot silently resolve a missing identity check or unpaid obligation.

At t+1, Sam opens the learning environment, finds the first teaching session and attends it. The course title, mode and compulsory first-stage modules match the version attached to the offer and enrolment. The tutor knows how to refer any remaining issue. The narrative ends with Sam able to begin learning, while later module choices, support, assessment and progression continue in their own stories.

## Actors, ownership and separate states

For this proposed fixture, **Admissions is one logical authority** for application assessment, offers, conditions and place confirmation. Whether that capability is hosted within Revelation or a connected admissions product remains an explicit deployment decision. It must not acquire competing writers. CRM owns the enquiry, visit and permitted relationship communications. SRS owns canonical student identity, the accepted student-record consequences, enrolment and curriculum binding. The integration boundary routes facts to their owner; it does not make academic judgements.

| Actor | Responsibility / authority | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Sam | Submit information, ask questions, respond through the appropriate channel, complete registration tasks. | Can request corrections/review; cannot self-confirm grades or authorise enrolment. |
| Recruitment adviser / event coordinator | Explain approved public information and arrange the visit. | Cannot make an academic offer or expose an access request to selection staff by default. |
| Aisha / academic admissions assessor | Explain the course and assess delegated academic criteria where appointed. | A subject talk is not a binding admissions decision. |
| Admissions officer / delegated confirmer | Own completeness, assessment, offer conditions and authorised confirmation. | Must use the current application/offer version and verified evidence. |
| UCAS / permitted qualification sources | Supply or receive the appropriate external admissions facts and evidence. | Actual channels, formats, directions and deadlines must be verified before implementation. |
| Registry / identity reviewer | Govern person linkage, prospective conversion and registration decisions. | Similarity is not sufficient authority for an identity merge; confirmed place is not active enrolment. |
| Finance / funding officer | Confirm the applicable fee/funding arrangement and eligible external notifications. | Student eligibility and payments are not inferred from an academic offer. |
| IAM, library, VLE, accommodation and teaching teams | Apply the authorised service entitlements or operational allocations they own. | One service's success cannot stand for every other's acknowledgement. |
| Integration/service desk owners | Reconcile delivery and applied state; explain failures and recover them. | Do not change an offer, mark or enrolment to bypass a service fault. |

| Record dimension | Main-path states | Evidence needed for the last transition |
|---|---|---|
| Enquiry/visit | Enquiry → booked → attended → follow-up complete | Event record and relevant communications, not academic attendance. |
| Application assessment | Received → complete → assessed | Required evidence and attributed assessment decision. |
| Fee status | Not assessed → assessed (home) | Evidence used and attributed fee-status decision under `FEES-2030@1`, stated on the offer. |
| Offer | Authorised conditional offer → issued/reconciled; Sam's firm response recorded separately | Exact offer version, official-channel receipt and response. |
| Conditions / place | Awaiting evidence → individually verified → authorised confirmed place | Verified grades, condition evaluation and confirmation authority; external state reconciled. |
| Registration | Prospective/prepared → checks pending → approved enrolment | Identity, academic/financial registration policy outcomes and Registry authority. |
| Access / arrival | Per-service pending → applied; separately not arrived → arrived/inducted | Each entitlement acknowledgement; separately campus/induction evidence. Neither substitutes for registration. |

## Closing evidence and interlocks

The evidence chain is: approved public-course reference → enquiry/visit → application source/version → assessed criteria → exact offer and official response → verified results/conditions → authorised place confirmation → governed identity conversion → registration decision and curriculum binding → service acknowledgements → separately recorded arrival/first teaching. Audit entries identify actor, role, time, reason and source/decision reference; personal evidence is restricted by purpose.

AJ-01 supplies the approved course and publication evidence. AJ-07 owns alternative offers, Clearing and deferral. AJ-16 owns detailed registration, fee/funding and access activation; AJ-17 owns campus services; AJ-18 owns actual module registration; AJ-19 owns the timetable; AJ-20 owns teaching rosters and tutor relationships. AJ-21 receives a separate support request if Sam makes one. AJ-34/37/38/39 provide regulatory, communication, records and exchange controls. Each subjourney supplies a receipt/outcome or an owned unresolved item, not an assumed success.

At this narrative's successful close, the six compulsory first-stage module registrations correspond to the shared fixture, core learning access is demonstrably usable and no mandatory registration check is unresolved. Other non-blocking tasks may remain only if their owner, next action and effect on Sam are visible. Failed or incomplete runs must end with their actual status, not a fictional arrival success.
