# AJ-10 — A university experience without campus attendance

Status: **Proposed narrative v0.8**, 14 November 2026 · [Document status](README.md#status-of-these-documents) · [Flexible-learning fixture](flexible-learning-and-service-fixture.md) · [Access support](aj-36-access-and-service-support.md)

**Perspective:** Farah, an online tutor, a digital librarian and an assessment officer. **Boundary:** remote entry and usable learning through one confirmed module result and an approved continuing plan. **Timeline:** tX-16 to tX+26; tX = 20 September 2032 = t+104.

## The story

### The offer describes an achievable online course — tX-16 to tX-8

Farah works in Dubai and wants to study without relocating. The approved online master's specification distinguishes recorded learning, scheduled tutorials, assessment windows and available support. Admissions checks the academic evidence and the approved route, while the course team discusses workload and time zones. An online course is not a promise that every activity can happen at any time.

Farah accepts the separate 180-credit programme in the fixture. The agreed plan is 40, 60 and 80 credits over three academic years. Year 3 combines the sixth taught module with the 60-credit dissertation, which Farah can begin only after at least 100 taught credits are confirmed. This is not Mei's full-time master's or a variation of Sam's undergraduate diet. The offer records the assessed fees and applicable terms; it does not invent a sponsor requirement merely because Farah lives abroad. Any applicable territorial, professional or resource restriction must be checked before the offer promises access.

### Remote identity and usable access are different gates — tX-8 to tX+1

An approved remote identity process returns a verification outcome. An inconclusive check goes to an authorised reviewer with an accessible alternative route; it does not create a second identity or cause automatic rejection. Registry completes the other registration gates and confirms Farah's route and first module. Library staff check remote resource entitlement and permitted use from Farah's location, including an accessible alternative for an unavailable licensed item.

An accepted registration message reaches IAM, but the VLE lacks Farah's module role. [AJ-36](aj-36-access-and-service-support.md) resolves this specific fault by tX, without re-enrolling her. Farah verifies the learning area, an accessible sample resource, library access and the help route before teaching at tX+1. A successful login alone would not prove that the course is usable. No accommodation booking, campus-card collection or physical arrival is required for this route.

### Engagement reflects the agreed learning design — tX+1 to tX+9

The tutor and Farah agree how activity, portfolio milestones and contact will demonstrate engagement. The schedule states the original time zone and displays local times; a recording has an equivalent learning activity where permitted. The attendance service uses that approved schedule, rather than treating an empty classroom seat as an absence.

The tutor reviews missed agreed activities with Farah and checks access faults or approved adjustments first. A download count cannot establish understanding, and an overnight login pattern cannot establish risk. A concern follows [AJ-23's human review](aj-23-engagement-and-intervention.md), using the native attendance module's evidence/case boundary. A separate external-provider variant needs its own confirmed-outcome contract. No raw VLE clickstream becomes a core academic decision.

### An inaccessible resource needs an authorised assessment response — tX+10 to tX+11

Farah reports that an essential assessment resource cannot be used with her access setup. The tutor reproduces the fault, supplies an equivalent accessible resource and records the incident. The assessment officer reviews the impact before the deadline and approves a replacement first-attempt deadline under `ONLINE-DEADLINE-2032@1`.

The original deadline is 29 November 2032, 16:00 UTC, displayed as 20:00 in Asia/Dubai. The approved replacement is 6 December at the same times. Both remain in the history, linked to the officer's decision and applied VLE acknowledgement. An unsent request or an IT ticket is not an extension. A longer-term adjustment follows [AJ-21](aj-21-reasonable-adjustments.md); an unrelated personal disruption follows [AJ-22](aj-22-exceptional-circumstances.md), with separate evidence and authority.

Farah submits by the replacement deadline and receives a timestamped receipt. If the portal is unavailable, an approved contingency submission route must preserve the work version and receipt; staff do not invent an on-time submission afterwards.

### A submission becomes an academic result — tX+18 to tX+25

The assessor marks ONL710's portfolio at 62 at tX+18. Independent moderation at +20 confirms the proposed result against its own level-7 criteria. The designated online-course board ratifies at +24 and publication follows at +25, using [AJ-24](aj-24-assessment-and-results.md) and [AJ-25](aj-25-exam-board-and-ratification.md) controls with this programme's supplied regulations. These dates do not reuse the undergraduate EDS board.

The pass threshold is 50, so the confirmed result earns 20 credits. The VLE's earlier mark was provisional, and publication is not a whole-course award. Farah sees the result, feedback and review route. A suspected integrity issue would need its separately authorised outcome before the affected decision; geographical distance does not justify an automatic adverse judgement.

### Flexibility is an approved plan, not unbounded enrolment — tX+26

The adviser confirms 20 earned and 160 remaining credits. Farah plans the second 20-credit module for the rest of the first year; offering dates, capacity, prerequisites, resource access and affordability are checked before registration. The remaining-year plan still meets the approved 40/60/80 structure, but future results are not preloaded.

If work commitments require a lower load, the adviser obtains an authorised revised plan and reviews fees, maximum study duration and any service consequences. An interruption uses [AJ-28](aj-28-study-change-and-return.md); continued access during a pause has its own scope and dates. Farah closes this story with a real first-module outcome and a feasible next step, not an inferred master's degree.

## Starting pack and authority

Supply `ONLINE-2032@1`, `ONLINE-DEADLINE-2032@1`, approved definitions and offerings, verified admission/identity/financial outcomes, Farah's declared time zone, access tests and the synthetic incident/work evidence as it occurs. Admissions owns entry, Registry owns registration, IAM/library/VLE own access application, support specialists own adjustments, and designated academic authorities own assessment changes and results. A tutor's helpful response cannot replace a formal deadline decision.
