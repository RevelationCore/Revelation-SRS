# AJ-30 — A funded project creates a doctoral opportunity

Status: **Proposed narrative v0.8**, 14 November 2026 · [Document status](README.md#status-of-these-documents) · [Research fixture](research-and-partnership-fixture.md) · [Supervision and progress](aj-31-research-supervision-and-progress.md)

**Perspective:** Leila as principal investigator, Morgan as applicant, a funding officer and Research Admissions. **Boundary:** project proposal and studentship funding through separately authorised admission, supervision and registration readiness. **Timeline:** tR-40 to tR+1, where **tR = 22 September 2031 = t+52**. Dates are fictional institutional planning windows.

## The story

### Funding starts with a proposal — tR-40 to tR-28

Leila develops an environmental-data research proposal and asks the funding officer to check its budget, student support, facilities and sponsor conditions. Research Proposals records the proposal revision, reviewers and funder decision. A promising application is not an awarded grant, and approval to spend on equipment does not imply permission to appoint a student.

The approved award creates studentship `STUDSHIP-MORGAN-001` linked to `GRANT-ENV-2031-01`. The name is a fixture label; at this point no candidate has been selected. The award specifies one funded place, fee support, the illustrative stipend schedule and eligibility conditions. Research Admissions receives the permitted award facts and references, not an assumption that a grant record is a student record.

### Morgan applies to the opportunity — tR-24 to tR-16

Morgan submits an application and verified prior qualifications, a research statement and required references. Admissions resolves identity against existing records and gives Morgan an accessible means to complete missing information. Research fit, academic eligibility and any funder conditions are assessed with their own evidence.

Leila participates within her appointed selection role, but cannot unilaterally admit someone because she holds the grant. An authorised selection panel records its assessment and conflicts. The application may be returned for clarification without being recorded as an academic rejection. A reserve candidate is not simultaneously allocated the same funded place.

### An offer names the distinct commitments — tR-12 to tR-8

Research Admissions approves an offer identifying the programme, proposed project, entry date, academic conditions, fee position and separate studentship terms. Morgan can distinguish the university's offer from the funder's conditions. A conditional funding decision is labelled as such; the interface does not promise an unconditional stipend.

Morgan accepts through the permitted route. The funding officer links the accepted candidate to the one available studentship, checks the current award version and records the commitment. A duplicated acceptance returns the existing allocation. If the grant changes or is withdrawn, the case returns for a documented funding/offer decision and communication; it is not silently converted to self-funding.

### Suitable supervision must actually be approved — tR-6 to tR-2

Leila and Evan are nominated as the supervisory team. AJ-31 checks HR eligibility, capacity, conflicts and academic suitability and obtains the PGR Director's decision at tR-3. Admissions consumes that decision, not merely the names printed on the proposal. A supervisor's imminent departure or excessive load remains a blocker until a suitable team is authorised.

The academic and funding conditions are verified against actual evidence. Morgan's main fixture has right-to-study readiness without a sponsor case; a sponsored alternative must use AJ-09's approved process and current external policy inputs. No nationality-based shortcut is applied.

### Registration and payment readiness remain separate — tR-1 to tR+1

Registry consumes the accepted offer, verified conditions, approved team and financial-readiness decision through AJ-16. Registration is authorised before the effective candidature start at tR. An account or CRIS profile created earlier is not evidence that research has begun. Morgan completes induction at tR+1 and sees the actual supervisory contact, training tasks and project permissions.

The funding/payment owner schedules only eligible authorised stipend instructions. A £64,800 budget is not proof that any money has reached Morgan. AJ-32 reconciles payment and separates later teaching salary from the studentship. The research profile records its source candidature and approved project without exposing private application evidence.

If Morgan declines before registration, Admissions closes the offer and the funding owner releases the reservation under the award conditions. They retain the decision history and reconcile any premature service provisioning. A replacement candidate needs a new selection and allocation decision, not a renamed enrolment.

## Starting pack and authority

Supply `PGR-FUND-2031@1`, `PGR-ADMIT-2031@1`, a synthetic award/conditions bundle, approved programme and project definition, selection criteria, candidate evidence and staff eligibility sources. Supply decisions only when their scene occurs. Research Proposals owns the award; Admissions owns admission; the PGR Director owns supervision; Registry owns registration; Finance/Payroll own the relevant financial effects. Grant, candidature and payment identities remain separate and linked.
