# AJ-11 — Learning across employment and university

Status: **Proposed narrative v0.7**, 14 November 2026 · [Document status](README.md#status-of-these-documents) · [Ada's fixture](research-and-partnership-fixture.md) · [Study changes](aj-28-study-change-and-return.md)

**Perspective:** Ada, an employer mentor, the apprenticeship administrator and the academic programme lead. **Boundary:** agreed learning/employment/funding responsibilities through university study, employer change and separately evidenced academic and occupational completion. **Timeline:** entry tX-16 to tX+1; employer change tX+20 to tX+24; later completion uses a separately supplied final-stage snapshot. tX = 20 September 2032 = t+104.

## The story

### Three parties agree a workable plan — tX-16 to tX-8

Ada applies to the separately approved `NBU-DATAAPP@1.0` route while employed by Westmere Analytics. The university assesses academic eligibility and prior learning; the apprenticeship team verifies employment/funding applicability under supplied rules. An employer's willingness to pay cannot bypass academic entry criteria, and an academic offer does not establish an eligible apprenticeship arrangement.

Ada, the employer and the university review the learning plan, scheduled university and workplace activity, support, assessment responsibilities and review points. The approved 360-credit academic route is distinct from the fictional occupational standard `OCC-DATA@1`. Any recognition of previous learning uses evidence and approval equivalent to AJ-08, rather than equating years in a job with credits.

### Register the academic route and keep funding visible — tX-6 to tX+1

The agreement records who supplies employment evidence, pays which obligations, signs learning activity and communicates changes. Finance approves the arrangement and Registry authorises enrolment through AJ-16. Module selections use the approved route and actual capacity. Employer contact details do not grant access to all of Ada's personal or academic records.

Ada starts teaching at tX+1 and confirms workplace supervision and learning opportunities. The university and employer calendars are reconciled; a campus closure does not automatically excuse employment or mean qualifying learning occurred. The student view distinguishes planned hours, verified activity, assessed evidence, funding status and earned credit.

### Review evidence, not just attendance totals — tX+8 to tX+12

A tripartite review examines module progress and verified workplace learning against the plan. Ada identifies that a planned activity is unavailable in the current role. The mentor and programme lead agree a feasible alternative within the approved outcomes, or seek a formal plan change if needed. A manager cannot waive a compulsory academic outcome.

The administrator records evidence and actions with the right visibility. An unverified workplace log stays pending; repeated import cannot turn it into a second block of learning. A welfare or reasonable-adjustment request follows AJ-21/23 with minimum necessary employer instructions, not disclosure of the specialist evidence file.

### Ada changes employer — tX+20 to tX+24

Ada receives an offer from Brookfield Systems and tells the university before the proposed change. The case inventories the old and new employment dates, training responsibilities, remaining learning opportunities, funding agreement and any contractual liabilities. The academic enrolment does not transfer institutions merely because the employer changes.

The main branch has no gap: the old arrangement ends immediately before the new approved arrangement starts at tX+24. Before that point, designated owners verify the new employer's eligibility and capacity, agree a replacement mentor, approve the learning-plan changes and authorise funding/financial effects. Exact dates and counterpart acknowledgements prevent two employers claiming the same period.

Old employer access ends at its authorised boundary while historic attestations remain valid evidence of activity actually delivered. Brookfield receives only its permitted context and future responsibilities, not automatic access to unrelated past employment or support records. Ada retains the academic results and module bindings already earned.

### A break in employment is a different branch

If new employment is not ready, the administrator applies the supplied break/funding policy and obtains an explicit decision on academic continuation, pause or another route. No fixed permissible break duration is invented here. The case gives Ada an owner, review date and clear explanation of what study/access can continue.

AJ-28 owns any academic status change; funding bodies and employers retain their own decisions. Continued access is not evidence that funding claims remain eligible. A late response or rejected claim opens reconciliation without deleting Ada's academic history or silently charging a different payer.

### Completion has more than one authority

In a separately supplied final-stage snapshot, the academic board verifies the approved degree's credit and assessment requirements and recommends the university award. Any occupational assessment readiness and final result are independently evidenced under the agreed standard and responsible body. One result cannot be copied into the other.

A student can have a pending occupational outcome while an academic decision is already recorded, subject to the actual approved dependencies in the programme. The fixture explicitly requires both for apprenticeship completion but does not make the occupational decision a substitute for degree conferral. AJ-29 issues university documents only after its own authority; permitted completion claims go through AJ-34 with source lineage.

## Starting pack and authority

Supply `ADA-APP-2032@1`, approved academic/occupational requirements, employment and funding agreements, identity links, learning calendars, evidence/claim rules and designated signatories. Later completion scenes need actual final results and body decisions, not elapsed years. Academic authorities own admission/results; employment/funding owners approve their obligations; employer mentors attest relevant learning; Ada participates in plans and receives decisions. GI-05 interfaces are proposed, not asserted live services.
