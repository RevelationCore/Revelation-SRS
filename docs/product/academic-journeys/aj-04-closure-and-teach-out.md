# AJ-04 — Closing recruitment while honouring existing commitments

Status: **Proposed narrative v0.7**, 14 November 2026 · [Document status](README.md#status-of-these-documents) · [Established closure policy](change-and-exceptions-fixture.md#explicit-additional-policies) · [Programme review](aj-03-programme-review-and-improvement.md)

**Perspective:** the faculty dean, Jo, Admissions and the programme delivery team. **Boundary:** a closure proposal through authorised intake closure, individual commitments and maintained completion provision; historic academic records remain available. **Timeline:** t+76 to t+88, new-entry effect from t+156, and continuing annual review until every affected commitment is resolved. Use [t = 23 September 2030](README.md#timeline-convention).

## The story

### Closure has a scope — t+76 to t+78

The faculty proposes closing new part-time EDS entry from 2033 because it cannot commit resources for new cohorts alongside its other provision. This expands the already agreed `CLOSE-EDS-PT-2033@1` decision; it does not close the full-time course or withdraw Jo. The dean identifies the exact route, intakes, proposed last entry and decision authority. The academic registrar confirms which measures of the student protection plan `SPP-NBU@1` the closure triggers, and the plan's owner signs off the resulting student communication.

Registry inventories active, interrupted and repeating students, accepted/deferred offers, shared modules and outstanding promises. Jo's 2031 part-time route remains bound to version 1, with its six-year planned sequence. The 2032 part-time intake is also protected. The fixture has no 2033 part-time applications or offers at the proposed decision point, but the inventory must prove that rather than assuming a blank future intake has no commitments.

### Funding existing study is part of the decision — t+78 to t+82

The programme team prepares a completion matrix showing each student's remaining modules, prerequisites, offering periods, assessment opportunities and support needs. Jo's later modules must remain deliverable after new entry closes. The plan includes approved resit/return windows and their resource implications; it is not limited to the normal last teaching date.

Shared-module owners confirm their commitments. Statistics version 1 and version 2 run in parallel from 2033/34 under AJ-02's conditions. Timetabling, staffing, library and Finance owners attest to actual required resources. If a compulsory module cannot be delivered, an equivalence or transfer proposal needs academic approval and any required student agreement before the closure can be presented as a safe completion plan.

The dean consults affected students and records responses and resulting changes. Jo can see what remains available and who owns a problem, without being promised a new curriculum or a guaranteed future pass. Completion forecasts are planning assumptions until actual results and registrations exist.

### Approve closure and publish the instruction — t+84 to t+88

At t+84 the panel approving the future EDS release, with the admissions policy owner's agreement, authorises the explicit closure instruction. The approved manifest includes its route/intake scope, effective applicability, resource commitments and exception ownership. Absence of the part-time route from a future release is not itself a closure event.

Before public publication at t+88, Admissions confirms that every application channel has disabled 2033-and-later part-time entry. The website states that 2033 entry is full-time only while retaining accurate information for existing students. A stale application channel remains an owned blocker to claiming closure is complete; hiding a web page is not enough.

Existing 2031/2032 students retain their academic definitions and authorised services. Registry does not cancel their modules or apply the new full-time diet because recruitment is closed. Accepted catalogue, public, admissions and student-facing states are reconciled separately.

### An offer-holder wants a different year — before the next intake

A 2032 part-time offer-holder requests deferral to 2033. Admissions explains that the requested part-time intake will not run and offers the permitted choices: retain the 2032 start, make a new full-time 2033 application, or be released. The officer does not silently transfer the offer or report the applicant as withdrawn from study they never began.

If the inventory instead discovers a pre-existing commitment to the closing intake, the case receives individual notice, review and a documented resolution under AJ-07. Any additional provision or alternative must have real authority and funding. An integration operator cannot solve the discrepancy by deleting the offer.

### Teach-out remains a managed obligation — t+156 onwards

The planning horizon is explicit. Jo's six-year plan ends in 2036/37. A 2032 part-time entrant on the same plan finishes in 2037/38, or in 2039/40 after the two-year maximum interruption under `CHANGE-2030@1`. Remaining provision is therefore committed to the end of 2039/40 unless every affected student has completed, transferred or withdrawn earlier. The dean and Registry conduct each annual review and record any change to that date. Each annual review checks actual remaining students, outstanding results, return dates and resource commitments. Jo is not forced to finish on the original plan if an authorised interruption changes it. Any revised completion route uses AJ-28's authority and version mapping, with recalculated funding and delivery responsibilities.

The programme's operational retirement is permitted only after all relevant obligations and remaining cases have been resolved or explicitly transferred under authority. Historic specifications, marks, decisions and awards remain reconstructible under AJ-38. Recruitment closure, final delivery, end of support and records disposition are different milestones.

A separate whole-programme variant applies the same controls to all routes, including shared-module effects on other programmes. It requires its own scope and approval; the part-time decision cannot be reused to close the whole EDS programme.

## Starting pack and authority

Supply the existing closure instruction/policy, the student protection plan `SPP-NBU@1`, exact affected intakes, verified commitment inventory, each remaining study plan, funded resource attestations and channel list. Jo's original plan is supplied unchanged. The dean proposes, academic authorities approve completion/transition rules, the panel and Admissions authorise closure, resource owners commit delivery and each channel owner attests application. The last student's departure does not itself authorise record deletion.
