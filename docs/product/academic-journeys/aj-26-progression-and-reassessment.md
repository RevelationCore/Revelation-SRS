# AJ-26 — Progressing, resitting or repeating

Status: **Proposed narrative v0.6**, 13 November 2026 · [Document status](README.md#status-of-these-documents) · [Shared rules and marks](taught-student-fixture.md) · [Award completion](aj-29-awards-graduation-and-alumni.md)

**Perspective:** Sam, Ben, a progression adviser and Registry. **Boundary:** a ratified academic decision becomes an understandable, authorised next-study or reassessment plan. Annual registration, actual selection and later ratification remain separate actions. **Timeline:** t+40 to t+51, t+92 to t+103, and Ben's t+144 to t+151 branch. Use the [shared t](README.md#timeline-convention).

## The story

### Sam knows what progression permits — t+40

Sam opens the published first-stage result. All 120 credits are passed, and the board has authorised progression. The adviser explains the next steps: choose a permissible stage-2 plan and complete annual registration. The message does not claim that options are already allocated or that next-year financial tasks are complete.

The progression plan refers to the exact ratified result, course/diet and policy versions. Sam can trace a prerequisite decision back to the passed module. A provisional calculation or a message from a lecturer cannot substitute for the board authority required by this fixture.

### Study choices and annual obligations reconnect — t+40 to t+51

Sam chooses the shared fixture's options in AJ-18. The capacity issue in Community Field Study has an operational owner and is resolved at t+49. Registry separately confirms annual registration and its dates. The services then prepare the correct next-year access; the original enrolment identity and historical study remain intact.

At the end of stage 2, the process repeats against t+91 ratification and t+92 publication. Sam's passed `EDS570` supports final-year Applied Ecology. The programme has not silently switched diets between years. A returner whose required module is no longer offered needs an approved equivalent/transition and impact review, not a convenient substitution hidden in registration.

### Ben needs a specific reassessment plan — t+144 to t+145

Ben's final board result says the ethics component failed. The adviser explains why a project aggregate of 63 did not pass the mandatory component. Ben can see the original component marks, rule and decision, how to seek review and exactly what further work is authorised.

The plan creates one permitted additional ethics attempt under `REASSESS-EDS-2030@1`. The passed report mark of 70 is carried forward with its provenance. The plan records the component, assessment method, deadline, maximum attempt and mark-cap rule. Ben is told before the attempt that a capped project counts at 40 towards his classification. It is not a new full project requiring the report to be marked again. Any different method would need an approved reassessment pattern before it could be used.

The learning/exam services acknowledge the new attempt and necessary access. Ben remains on the reassessment route; he is not recorded as a graduate or sent Sam's confirmed ceremony invitation. A permitted late submission or exceptional circumstance needs its own authority, not an informal adviser promise.

### The new attempt changes the outcome, not the past — t+148 to t+151

Ben completes the ethics reassessment and receives 50. With the carried report, the raw recomputed project mark is 66. The independent ethics hurdle now passes. Under the explicit fixture policy the recorded reassessed module result is capped at 40. The new result records both values, the attempt linkage and the unchanged report source.

AJ-24 moderates the new set at t+149, and the supplementary board ratifies at t+150 before publication at t+151. The project earns 40 credits once; it does not earn another 40 for the repeated component. The original failed attempt remains visible to authorised readers. AJ-29 then classifies Ben's complete record, with the project counted at its capped 40.

### Not every branch ends in immediate progression

If the further component attempt also fails, the stated fixture permits no automatic third attempt. The authorised academic team must consider a separately governed repeat, transfer or exit route using its own policy. An exit diploma is not inferred from “enough credits” without the required award definition and authority. The student receives a reason, owner and review route while unresolved decisions stay pending.

A correction to a previous ratified result can affect a plan already in motion. The correction owner identifies registrations, finance and services needing reconciliation. The adviser does not erase the earlier plan or let a stale progression event restore superseded eligibility.

## Starting pack and authority

Sam's first snapshot contains the t+39 ratified stage-1 decision; stage 2 uses its own later decision. Ben's snapshot contains his actual failed project attempt, carried report, complete prior-credit references and the authorised reassessment decision. Do not preload a passing retake. The shared policy fixes this branch's threshold, cap and attempt count.

The board owns academic progression/reassessment authority; advisers explain and coordinate; Registry creates the approved plan and registration consequences; assessment staff own the new attempt; Finance owns any separately authorised liability. An adviser cannot waive a must-pass hurdle or grant an extra attempt outside their authority.
