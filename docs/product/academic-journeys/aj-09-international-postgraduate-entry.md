# AJ-09 — From overseas offer to supported study

Status: **Proposed narrative v0.8**, 14 November 2026 · [Document status](README.md#status-of-these-documents) · [Mei's fixture](research-and-partnership-fixture.md) · [Registration](aj-16-registration-and-entitlements.md)

**Perspective:** Mei, an international admissions adviser, an authorised sponsor-compliance officer and Registry. **Boundary:** direct taught-postgraduate application through separately evidenced admission, sponsorship/right-to-study and registration, then supported arrival and engagement. **Timeline:** tX-32 to tX+6, where tX = 20 September 2032 = t+104. All deadlines and external eligibility rules are supplied policy inputs, not legal guidance in this fiction.

## The story

### Mei compares a specific course — tX-32 to tX-28

Mei reads the approved `NBU-ENVMSC@1.0` specification, including modules, dissertation, delivery, fees and start date. The direct application references that version and the intended intake. The adviser explains how academic conditions, financial readiness, sponsorship and final registration differ; a web enquiry is none of those decisions.

Mei submits qualification evidence and the other required materials through a permitted accessible route. Admissions verifies source authenticity and records any evaluated equivalence under the configured policy. A translation or language-evidence requirement is explicit and reviewed, not inferred from a surname or nationality. Identity uncertainty becomes an owned case rather than an automatic merge.

### Offer conditions are discharged by evidence — tX-24 to tX-16

An authorised academic assessor makes a recommendation. Admissions issues an offer with exact course/intake, conditions, fee-status decision, terms and relevant timelines. Mei accepts, but a pending qualification verification remains visible until the approved evidence is received.

The Finance adviser separately checks the funding/payment arrangement and any required financial evidence. Neither fee status nor a successful payment proves academic eligibility or permission to study. An unavailable verification service produces a specified manual-review or pending route, not a fabricated pass. Changed conditions or dates require a versioned offer and communication.

### Sponsorship is a separately authorised process — tX-12 to tX-6

The sponsor officer determines the applicable route using the actual case and current approved policy. Mei's fixture includes a sponsorship requirement and a verified eligibility decision. The officer reviews the exact course, identity, offer conditions, required evidence and existing sponsorship identifiers before authorising the relevant CAS request/assignment process.

The external service or controlled manual channel returns its attributable response. SRS stores the permitted identifier/status and provenance. A request sent is not a CAS assigned; an assigned CAS is not an immigration decision or registration approval. The workflow cannot derive sponsorship solely from nationality, a deposit or an attendance flag.

If the response is lost, the operator reconciles the same request before retrying. Duplicate sponsorship identifiers or conflicting external states require the designated specialist review. No live submission or real identifier is generated by this narrative.

### Travel and study readiness do not always agree — tX-4 to tX-1

Mei supplies the required decision/right-to-study evidence and expected arrival information. Registry verifies the permitted status under the applicable policy and completes the ordinary academic/financial registration checks. Housing and orientation plans are separately reconciled. The course team confirms access and first teaching at tX+1.

In a delayed-arrival branch, the adviser checks the authorised latest-start/participation rules, missed teaching and sponsor implications before offering a revised plan. Deferral requires an accepted future intake and new relevant authority; the system cannot move the date and retain every old approval unchanged. In a refusal branch, the sponsor/admissions owners explain permitted next steps and financial/service consequences without inventing a guaranteed appeal, refund or future permission.

### Arrival starts a new evidence period — tX to tX+2

Mei arrives, completes the required in-person or approved equivalent checks and attends induction. Actual study-start/engagement evidence is recorded by its owner. Advance registration or an accommodation check-in cannot stand in for that evidence.

The student view shows the confirmed course, applicable tasks and support contacts in accessible language. A request for ongoing study support enters AJ-21 with its own evidence and approval, not a flag copied from immigration documents. Identity and sponsorship records remain restricted; lecturers receive only what they need to deliver teaching.

### Engagement concerns lead to human review — tX+3 to tX+6

`ENGAGEMENT-2030@1` carries forward unchanged for 2032/33. A missed session triggers that attendance process only when its conditions are met. The caseworker verifies timetable/roster accuracy, approved operational adjustments and contact with Mei. A corrected record can resolve the alert. Genuine concerns receive an owned support or status review.

Where sponsor action is relevant, the specialist receives a fixed evidence snapshot and makes the separately authorised decision. AJ-34 handles the appropriate reporting obligation and receiver response. A risk score, tutor email or automated timer cannot withdraw sponsorship or academic enrolment. Later interruption or course transfer uses AJ-28 and fresh sponsor assessment rather than reusing the original admission decision.

## Starting pack and authority

Supply `MEI-PGT-2032@1`, `ENGAGEMENT-2030@1`, approved master's specifications and entry criteria, verified applicant evidence, fee/funding policy and an institution-approved sponsor/right-to-study checklist and channel contract. Any real deployment must supply current rules; the fixture asserts no national thresholds or deadlines. Academic/Admissions staff own admission, specialist sponsor staff own sponsorship decisions, Registry owns enrolment, Finance owns payments and support/Attendance own their separate cases.
