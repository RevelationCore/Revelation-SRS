# AJ-22 — A disruption to a particular assessment

Status: **Proposed narrative v0.6**, 13 November 2026 · [Document status](README.md#status-of-these-documents) · [Extension fixture](change-and-exceptions-fixture.md) · [Reasonable adjustments](aj-21-reasonable-adjustments.md)

**Perspective:** Leah, a specialist case adviser, an independent approver and the board secretary. **Boundary:** a time-bound claim through a reasoned determination, separately authorised academic effect and acknowledged assessment arrangements. **Timeline:** t+29 to t+51, using [t = 23 September 2030](README.md#timeline-convention).

## The story

### Leah explains what happened — t+29 to t+30

A short personal disruption prevents Leah attending her scheduled Statistics exam at t+30. She opens the exceptional-circumstances route, identifies `EX-LEAH-SCI440-01` and receives an accessible explanation of the evidence, deadline and available help. The case is `EC-LEAH-001`. No diagnosis or real personal document is needed in the synthetic fixture.

The adviser checks identity and assessment linkage and records receipt against the configured clock. Leah initially omits the affected date from her statement. The request returns for that specific clarification while preserving its original receipt time. A permitted helper has scoped authority; their involvement does not grant access to Leah's whole record. An overdue claim would receive an admissibility decision rather than disappear from the queue.

### The determination has a precise scope — t+31 to t+32

The adviser assesses the evidence; a separately authorised approver accepts that the stated disruption affected this exam. The decision identifies the assessment, dates, reason category and permitted disclosure. Private evidence remains in the specialist service. The decision is not a mark, a grant of credit or an ongoing adjustment.

Leah sees that the claim is accepted and that an academic decision is still required. An unsuccessful claim would include reasons and a review route. Neither outcome creates an attendance exemption for unrelated teaching or alters Nina's separate support arrangements.

### The board makes the academic decision — t+36 to t+40

The approved operational determination reaches SRS and the relevant board pack. The secretary checks its version and scope without attaching the private evidence bundle. The assessment record distinguishes the affected absence from a confirmed failed attempt; missing evidence cannot be converted into a zero to complete the pack.

At t+39 the board authorises a replacement first attempt under `EC-2030@1`. Leah's final module/progression decision is deferred pending that evidence; unaffected candidates, including Sam, can receive their normal outcomes. Publication at t+40 explains the attempt, dates and next steps. Acceptance of circumstances has not itself passed Leah's module.

### A usable assessment opportunity — t+40 to t+47

Registry and the examinations owner create the linked replacement attempt for t+44. The original affected attempt remains traceable and is not counted as an exhausted failed attempt under this fixture policy. The new attempt is uncapped; it is distinguishable from Ben's later capped reassessment. Any independently approved adjustment would be applied using AJ-21's current instruction, not inferred from this claim.

Leah receives the arrangement and a correction route. A lost receiver acknowledgement triggers reconciliation before retry, not a second booking. In the numerical example she earns 58; the other five 20-credit stage-1 modules have separately ratified passes. AJ-24 moderates at t+45 and the supplementary board ratifies at t+46. At t+47 she receives the resulting 120-credit progression decision. Selection and annual registration remain separate tasks through t+51.

### Closing the case without losing the history

The adviser verifies that the determination, academic decision and delivery references agree. A later changed determination requires a new version and impact review; after ratification it uses AJ-27's amendment authority. The original evidence and decision stay under their retention and access rules. Case closure records completed or owned outstanding actions rather than implying every recipient has read a message.

## Starting pack and authority

Supply Leah's confirmed registration and exam entry, a restricted synthetic evidence bundle, the shared `EC-2030@1` policy, delegated roles and original attempt status. Introduce the mark of 58 and other passed results only in the later assessment snapshot. The specialist approver owns the determination; the board owns the academic effect; assessment/exams owners implement it; Registry publishes authorised outcomes. A caseworker cannot override a locked mark.
