## Shared-module change and retained delivery

`SCI440@1.0` stays valid for Sam, Nina, Ben, Leah, Priya, Omar and Jo's approved plans. In AJ-02 draft `MCP-SCI440-002/r1`, a new prerequisite of passed `SCI420` makes a first-year route that teaches both concurrently impossible. Reworked `r2` retains no new prerequisite and adds a supported introductory workshop within the module; approved outcomes and assessment criteria explicitly cover interpretation of statistical uncertainty. It remains level 4 and 20 credits. A separate fictional neighbouring programme, `NBU-GEO@1.0`, also uses `SCI440@1.0`; its dependency and concurrent scheduling are supplied in the impact snapshot, not invented enrolments or claims about a real programme.

AJ-02 approves the exact `SCI440@2.0` dependency at t+72 and delivers it to AJ-03 at t+74. Approval alone does not make it selectable. AJ-03 owns the complete 2033 programme release. Geography remains on version 1 until its own authorised change; it is not silently included in EDS's release. Version 2 applies to nobody before t+156, so 2032/33 still has only the regular version-1 offering. From 2033/34 both versions run in parallel: EDS 2033 entrants take version 2, while Geography and any EDS student repeating or resitting under an earlier binding take version 1. The delivery owner must fund distinct version-1 assessment and teaching arrangements from that year. Omar's 2032/33 return uses the regular version-1 offering; a variant in which he needs a further `SCI440` attempt in 2033/34 tests the parallel delivery. Equivalence to version 2 is a separate reviewed alternative, not an automatic substitution.
