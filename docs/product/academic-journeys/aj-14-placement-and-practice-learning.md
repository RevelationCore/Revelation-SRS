# AJ-14 — Learning in practice

Status: **Proposed narrative v0.7**, 14 November 2026 · [Document status](README.md#status-of-these-documents) · [Placement fixture](research-and-partnership-fixture.md) · [Assessment](aj-24-assessment-and-results.md)

**Perspective:** Rina, a placement coordinator, a host supervisor and the university's practice-learning lead. **Boundary:** approved placement and readiness through supervised evidence, replacement when delivery fails, and a separately authorised academic result. **Timeline:** tX+4 to tX+30, with moderation at tX+32, ratification at tX+40 and publication at tX+41, where tX = 20 September 2032 = t+104 is Rina's placement-year anchor. Her original entry at t+52 remains in her enrolment history.

## The story

### Match a placement to approved learning — tX+4 to tX+6

Rina has completed the required preceding study on the separately approved practice-based route. The coordinator matches her to Eastbank Service for `PRAC520@1.0`. The match considers the module's outcomes, supervision, location, capacity, accessibility and actual timetable. A host's available seat alone does not establish suitable learning.

The host agreement states responsibilities, permitted activities, supervision and escalation, assessment evidence and records handling. Rina receives the placement plan and understands which commitments are provisional. The fixture's four competencies and 120 verified supervised hours are distinct requirements; neither is a proxy for the other.

### Readiness is specific and dated — tX+6 to tX+7

The authorised owners verify the required clearance, training and operational readiness under the supplied placement policy. The host receives only permitted clearance status, scope and expiry, not the entire underlying personal evidence file. An outstanding check stays pending with its owner; a coordinator cannot mark it complete to meet a start-date target.

An approved support instruction, if present, comes through AJ-21 and is translated into practical arrangements with Rina and the host. It does not waive a competency or permit unsafe practice. Rina begins only once the required placement, supervision and readiness decisions are in place.

### Supervised evidence accumulates — tX+7 to tX+10

The supervisor records attributable activity and observations against each criterion. Rina can review the record and flag an incorrect entry. The first host supplies 40 verified hours and associated competency evidence. A time log does not become an assessed pass until the authorised assessor evaluates it.

The record identifies dates, assessor and setting so overlapping shifts, unsupported hours or duplicate uploads can be detected. Academic staff see the evidence needed for assessment; unrelated confidential service information is excluded. No real patient or client material is needed in this synthetic fixture.

### A supervision failure stops further activity — tX+10

The agreed supervision becomes unavailable and the host cannot safely provide the required activity. Rina reports the problem through the designated route. The placement lead pauses the affected activity, confirms immediate support and notifies the academic coordinator. This is not automatically an academic failure, disciplinary finding or withdrawal from the university.

The university reviews the evidence already obtained. In the main fixture the earlier 40 hours are valid and retained; later unsupported activity is not counted. If the investigation casts doubt on earlier evidence, that scope receives a separate assessment decision. A serious welfare/safety issue goes to the designated urgent responders without waiting for the routine replacement meeting.

### Replacement preserves learning and history — tX+16 to tX+28

After the campus closure period, a replacement at Lakeside is approved at tX+16. Its own supervisors, readiness checks, calendar and remaining learning opportunities are verified; Eastbank's approval does not transfer automatically. Rina resumes at tX+17, with an explicit plan for the remaining 80 hours and outstanding competencies.

The old allocation is end-dated and the new one linked. Financial/travel/support consequences are reviewed by their owners. The change does not create a second academic registration or erase the first host's valid evidence. By tX+28, the verified total is 40 + 80 = 120 hours and the supplied observations support all four competencies.

### The university decides the academic effect — tX+28 to tX+30

The university assessor reviews the combined portfolio against the approved criteria and confirms a proposed pass. Moderation follows AJ-24 at tX+32; the board ratifies under AJ-25 at tX+40 and the result is published at tX+41. The proposed 20 credits are not counted as earned simply because the host signs a completion form.

In a separate branch, Rina has 120 hours but lacks evidence for escalation of concerns; the module remains incomplete or receives the authorised outcome under its assessment rules. A professionally regulated-course variant additionally requires its actual professional standards, clearance rules and regulator relationships. Neither this fictional competency list nor a university module pass establishes professional registration.

## Starting pack and authority

Supply `PLACEMENT-2032@1`, Rina's actual prior results/registration, approved module and criteria, host agreements/calendars, readiness statuses, scoped assessors and synthetic observations. Supply the 40/80-hour evidence only as delivered. The host owns operational supervision and source observations, the placement lead owns allocation/safety escalation, specialists own clearances, and university academic authorities own results and credit. A host manager cannot amend programme rules.
