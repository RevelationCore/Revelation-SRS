# AJ-13 — A semester away that counts

Status: **Proposed narrative v0.8**, 14 November 2026 · [Document status](README.md#status-of-these-documents) · [Mobility fixture](flexible-learning-and-service-fixture.md) · [Prior learning](aj-08-part-time-entry-and-prior-learning.md)

**Perspective:** Luca, inbound visitor Tessa, the mobility coordinator and academic recognition assessors. **Boundary:** approved outbound/inbound plans through reconciled study evidence, credit decisions and dated service changes. **Timeline:** tX-20 to tX+27; tX = 20 September 2032 = t+104.

## The story

### Nomination does not establish equivalence — tX-20 to tX-12

Luca has completed the first stage of a separate geography programme and wants a semester at Alder University. The mobility coordinator checks the institutional agreement, host place and readiness requirements. Luca's director checks that the proposed study can satisfy the particular level-5 requirements in his route. Neither an available host place nor a similar module title establishes credit equivalence.

The academic authority approves `LA-LUCA-2032@1`, mapping three 10-unit host modules individually to three 20-credit home requirements. The supplied agreement defines the unit conversion and verifies level, outcomes and assessment; it is not a universal conversion formula. Funding, insurance, travel and any permission to study abroad are separately evidenced by their owners. Missing readiness can hold departure without deleting the academic plan.

### Two students have different institutional relationships — tX-8 to tX+1

Luca remains a Northbridge award student during the approved host period. His home study plan records mobility rather than withdrawal. The host confirms arrival and registration through the agreed channel; the coordinator checks them against the learning agreement. Accommodation and access owners receive actual absence/arrival dates and agreed service needs, not a generic command to close every account.

Separately, Tessa arrives from Alder as a visiting student. Her verified identity is linked to a dated visiting record and three approved 20-credit offerings with confirmed places. She receives the necessary library, learning and housing arrangements after their own checks. She does not become a candidate for Luca's Northbridge award. An existing Northbridge identity would be reviewed and reused rather than duplicated merely because the home-institution alias is new.

### A host timetable change returns to academic authority — tX+7 to tX+9

Alder cancels H201 before its planned teaching begins and offers H204. Luca asks the mobility coordinator to update the agreement. The academic assessor checks H204's outcomes, level, assessment and compatibility with the remaining route. The student and host confirm the feasible timetable, but their agreement alone cannot authorise home credit.

Revision 2 is approved at tX+8 before H204 teaching at +9. It explicitly replaces H201 against MOB501, retaining H202 and H203. The home plan, host acknowledgement and learner notice all identify revision 2. If no suitable replacement exists, the coordinator obtains an alternative study or return plan; a promise to “sort the credit out later” leaves a visible risk rather than a fictitious approval.

### The visiting record closes with usable evidence — tX+20 to tX+25

Tessa completes her Northbridge assessments at +20. Moderation at +21 and the authorised visiting-results board at +22 confirm 58, 61 and 64, all above the supplied pass threshold of 40. Results are published at +23 and the official 60-credit transcript is issued at +24 through [AJ-24/25 controls](aj-25-exam-board-and-ratification.md). Its issue version and delivery receipt are retained.

The visiting period ends at +23, while limited document access and service reconciliation continue to +25. Housing checks and library obligations have their own records. Alder decides how the transcript contributes to Tessa's home award; Northbridge neither confers that award nor assumes the host has applied the credit because a transcript was emailed. Outstanding reassessment would require a separate dated plan and access entitlement.

### An incomplete transcript cannot supply missing credit — tX+24 to tX+26

Luca returns with a permitted home timetable. A transcript at +24 contains H202 and H203 but omits H204. The mobility office verifies the source and requests the missing result; a screenshot from Luca cannot settle it. The 60-credit mobility block remains pending as a whole under this fixture. Any decision on partial credit would need a separately authorised branch.

Luca's remaining home modules run in the approved spring block for returning mobility students, from +24 to +40, because Alder's semester ends after Northbridge's second teaching period has begun. They can start at +24 because their supplied prerequisites do not depend on the pending block. This conditional registration authority is explicit; no missing credit is fabricated to make a prerequisite check pass. If a requested module did require the block, that registration would wait or need a separately approved exception.

At +26 Alder supplies an authenticated complete transcript showing Pass in H204, H202 and H203. The assessor matches the exact modules and period to agreement revision 2, checks there is no duplicate recognition, and retains the source document and verification reference.

### Recognition resumes the home route without inventing marks — tX+27

The academic recognition authority approves 30 host units as 60 home credits against MOB501/502/503, 20 each. The credit is recorded with its external source, agreement revision and decision. A replay of the same transcript cannot add another 60 credits. A later corrected transcript creates a reviewed successor, not a silent overwrite.

The Pass grades remain Pass; Northbridge does not manufacture a numeric average. A later award/classification must use separately supplied rules for recognised credit and the full result profile. Luca now has the mobility block satisfied and the remaining home stage-2 work still to complete. Tessa has a closed visit and an issued transcript. Neither student's success substitutes for the other's institutional decision.

## Starting pack and authority

Supply `MOBILITY-2032@1`, `VISIT-2032@1`, Luca's approved course and prior progression, both institutions' calendars, learning-agreement revisions, Tessa's offerings/capacities and verified transcript evidence when available. Host staff attest their own registration/results. Northbridge academics own equivalence and recognition; Registry owns home/visiting records; service owners own housing and access. Government or funding permissions, where applicable, need actual supplied rules and evidence; this fixture asserts none.
