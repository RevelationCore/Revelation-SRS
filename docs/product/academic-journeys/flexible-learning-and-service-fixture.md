## Evidence boundaries and review

The analytics scenario supplies AJ-03's earlier feedback pack; its corrected warehouse mapping does not silently repair AJ-34's separate collection transformation. AJ-37 consumes the approved release from AJ-03; it cannot approve curriculum. AJ-39 consumes authorised decisions from AJ-16/AJ-21/AJ-05; it cannot repeat their academic effects. Specify evidence only when its scene has occurred. Credentials, transcript verification, payments, acknowledgements and restore measurements need actual synthetic artefacts in a future implementation. Unassigned review owners: online learning and library, mobility and academic standards, continuing education and Finance, student representatives and analytics/privacy, IAM/security/service desk, communications, integration owners, Registry, records and platform operations.
