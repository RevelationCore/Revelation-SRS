# AJ-08 — Returning to education with prior learning

Status: **Proposed narrative v0.6**, 13 November 2026 · [Document status](README.md#status-of-these-documents) · [Jo's route and credit plan](change-and-exceptions-fixture.md#jos-route-and-remaining-credit) · [Module selection](aj-18-module-selection-and-diets.md)

**Perspective:** Jo, an admissions adviser, a recognition assessor and a part-time programme tutor. **Boundary:** direct application and evidenced prior learning through an approved remaining study plan and separately authorised registration. **Timeline:** tJ-32 to tJ+2, where **tJ = 22 September 2031 = t+52**; the later annual review is tJ+40 to tJ+52. Use the [extension's explicit conversion](change-and-exceptions-fixture.md#time-anchors-and-connected-chronology).

## The story

### Jo needs a workable route — tJ-32 to tJ-28

Jo contacts Northbridge about returning to study alongside work and caring commitments. The adviser shows the separately approved part-time EDS route, expected attendance and assessment demands, costs and support contacts. An individual timetable cannot be promised before actual offerings are checked.

Jo applies directly as `APP-JO-2031-01`. Identity matching checks for an existing person without assuming an old email uniquely identifies them. The intake starts on 22 September 2031. The approved release `REL-EDS-PT-2031-01` is a supplied starting condition; Admissions cannot create this route by accepting Jo onto the existing full-time one and changing a label.

### Evidence is assessed against named learning — tJ-27 to tJ-22

Jo requests recognition for previous programming study and relevant work. The assessor asks for a verified transcript and assessed portfolio. Initially the transcript is missing, so the claim remains pending. Jo's experience helps identify evidence; years in employment alone do not create academic credit.

Once verified, the evidence is mapped to the outcomes, level and assessment requirements of `SCI420@1.0`. The assessor recommends 20 credits and explains the comparison. A designated approver, independent of Jo and the recommendation where required by policy, records `RPL-JO-001@1`. The decision names the original qualification/evidence, equivalent module and any limits on reuse.

### Credit, exemption and marks are different facts — tJ-21 to tJ-18

The approved recognition counts once towards stage 1 and satisfies the named programming prerequisite under `RPL-PT-2031@1`. It is not a fabricated Northbridge mark, attendance record or second registration. The original 360-credit award requirement remains: 20 recognised plus 340 still to study.

The adviser checks the fixture's minimum 240 Northbridge credits and requirement to study all level-6 work there; Jo's proposed 340 meets those constraints. A request to recognise the compulsory ethics-bearing project would be rejected under this route's rule. If Jo disputes the mapping, a fresh review records its evidence and authority; an admissions officer cannot resolve it by silently adding credits.

### A six-year plan respects stage progression — tJ-18 to tJ-8

Jo and the tutor use the shared plan. Year 1 contains Environmental Systems, Mathematics and Statistics: 60 new credits, giving 80 including recognition if passed. Year 2 completes the remaining 40 stage-1 credits. Stage 2 spans years 3 and 4; stage 3 spans years 5 and 6, including the 40-credit project and its must-pass ethics component.

The plan is checked for prerequisites and real scheduled attendance. It does not label Jo a stage-2 student after the first 60-credit year. Annual continuation within a stage uses `PT-PROGRESSION-2031@1`; moving between stages still requires the whole 120 credits and academic authority. Future module passes are planning assumptions, not current evidence.

### Accept the offer, then register — tJ-8 to tJ-2

Admissions records the applicable fee-status decision and makes an offer naming the part-time route, recognition decision, remaining requirements and terms. Jo acknowledges the plan. Finance evaluates the prospective liability and funding arrangement using the updated pre-registration contract in AJ-16; no active enrolment is required merely to obtain that readiness decision.

Registry then checks identity, terms, confirmed place and financial readiness before approving registration. AJ-18 confirms the first-year 60-credit set using Jo's policy and recognised prerequisite evidence. Learning, library and timetable services apply the correct part-time context. None requires Jo to be marked as attending full time to make integration work.

### Revisit the plan as life and provision change — tJ to tJ+52

Jo starts teaching at tJ+1 and verifies access and the timetable. At the first annual review, the approved recognition and ratified results are checked separately. In the success snapshot, 60 newly earned plus 20 recognised credits allow continuation within stage 1; they do not complete it. The following year's actual offerings and registration obligations must still be confirmed.

A changed work schedule or unavailable future module opens an owned plan review. Any mode change, interruption or curriculum transition uses AJ-28 and retains the original recognition provenance. Jo does not inherit the 2033 full-time curriculum simply because it becomes the newest published course. No six-year plan guarantees a future award or class before its requirements are met.

## Starting pack and authority

Supply Jo's approved part-time release, intake, recognition and progression policies, actual synthetic prior-learning evidence, current offering/capacity data and scoped staff. Do not preload the recognition approval into the initial evidence-request scene. Admissions owns admission; qualified academic assessors/approvers own recognition; the programme owner owns feasible study planning; Registry and Finance retain separate authority. The complete plan is in the shared extension to prevent inconsistent copies.
