# AJ-29 — From completion to graduate

Status: **Proposed narrative v0.8**, 14 November 2026 · [Document status](README.md#status-of-these-documents) · [Shared marks and rules](taught-student-fixture.md) · [Exam board](aj-25-exam-board-and-ratification.md)

**Perspective:** Sam, the awards officer, ceremony organisers, records staff and alumni team. **Boundary:** a ratified recommendation through eligibility verification, separate award conferral, document issue and governed leaver services. **Timeline:** t+140 to t+156; Ben's later conferral branch begins after supplementary publication at t+151. Use the [same t](README.md#timeline-convention).

## The story

### Completion is demonstrated, not inferred — t+140 to t+144

The awards officer prepares Sam's candidate profile from the approved diet, actual registrations and ratified results. Every counted credit has provenance and appears once. The shared-module codes and project credit weighting are respected. A course brochure describing a 360-credit degree is not evidence that Sam has earned 360 credits.

The final board at t+143 ratifies the final outcomes and recommends Sam's award. Stage-2 mean 63⅓ and stage-3 mean 66.3 produce the fixture classification score 65.41, an upper second. The calculation retains its inputs, precision and policy. A rounded display value cannot change a borderline decision, and no undocumented discretion is added.

Ben's same board has authorised reassessment, not an award. The awards service cannot treat the high raw project aggregate as passed credit or include him in the confirmed graduation cohort. He has a clear next action through AJ-26 rather than an unexplained exclusion.

### An authorised officer confers the award — t+145

After the result release, the designated conferral authority checks Sam's complete profile, board recommendation and any relevant academic restrictions. They record the conferred award, its date, classification and source decision. The fixture separates this act from the earlier recommendation and from the ceremony.

An overdue library book or housing debt is a service matter under this scenario; it does not block the academic award. Any other purported hold needs explicit policy and proper authority, not an imported flag with an unclear consequence. Conferral is retry-safe: a lost response does not create a second degree.

### Documents are issued and remain verifiable — t+146

Sam confirms the permitted identity/display details for the certificate and receives the appropriate transcript and taught-programme achievement record. The document set references the conferred award and its issue version. EDRMS returns an archive receipt and retrievable reference; an email attachment alone is not proof that the official record was archived.

A corrected name or later academic amendment is handled through the appropriate governed process. The original issue remains traceable and any replacement is clearly identified. A reprint of the same document is distinguished from a changed award or a new issue version. The service does not overwrite a published certificate file in place and lose the audit trail.

### Sam chooses whether to attend — t+146 to t+150

The ceremony team invites eligible graduates to the specified event and asks about attendance, guests and practical accessibility. Sam accepts; another graduate may decline and still hold the conferred award. Booking and capacity decisions belong to the event service. The award date is not changed to match who walks across the stage.

Sam's guests receive only the information needed for attendance. They do not obtain access to marks, support evidence or financial records. A cancelled ceremony or undeliverable invitation creates an event/communication task, not academic revocation.

### Leaving preserves legitimate rights and records — t+145 to t+156

Registry records the academic completion/leaver status and authorised dates. IAM reviews student-role entitlements according to purpose and period, preserving any independent staff role. Library and accommodation owners resolve their obligations through AJ-17. Access needed to retrieve issued documents or pursue an authorised review has an explicit route; a sudden account deletion must not make the graduate's record unobtainable.

The alumni team opens or updates its relationship using permitted contact and preference information. Optional alumni marketing is separate from necessary award correspondence. Academic records and document history follow their retention/hold policies, not an indiscriminate deletion when student access expires.

### Ben completes later — t+151 onwards

The supplementary board's published outcome now records Ben's passed, capped project result and 40 project credits counted once. The awards officer assesses his complete profile: stage-2 mean 60 and stage-3 mean 53⅓, with the project counted at its recorded, capped 40. The score is 0.3 × 60 + 0.7 × 53⅓ = 55.33, a lower second, and the award is conferred at t+152. Ben's documents can explain the effect of the cap: counted at its raw 66, the score would have been 61.40. The narrative does not borrow Sam's classification.

Ben receives his own issued documents and a later ceremony opportunity. He is not retrospectively added to Sam's ceremony as if he had already qualified. In a variant where part of the profile is missing, the award case remains pending with the missing evidence and owner stated.

[AJ-33](aj-33-thesis-examination-and-award.md) supplies the distinct research examination, correction, deposit and completion authority for Morgan. It reuses common award/document services without a taught classification or HEAR, and [AJ-32](aj-32-student-employment-and-payments.md) preserves Morgan’s independently valid employment rights.

## Starting pack and authority

Supply Sam's full ratified profile and policy from the shared continuation, exact board recommendation, identity/display policy, conferral delegation, document template versions, archive/verification stubs, ceremony capacity/terms and leaver entitlement rules. Ben's branch uses his stage-2 and stage-3 profile from the shared continuation.

The exam board recommends under its authority; the designated awards authority confers; records staff issue/archive; ceremony staff manage attendance; service owners apply leaver effects; alumni staff manage their relationship. A ceremony booking, debt feed, document operator or event failure cannot independently create, alter or revoke an academic award.
