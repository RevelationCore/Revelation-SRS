# Data Subject Register

> Status: Draft — Phase 1
> Last updated: 2026-06-04
> This register documents every category of personal data held by Revelation SRS, the lawful basis for processing, the sensitivity classification, and the applicable retention period. It satisfies the requirement of UK GDPR Article 30 (Records of Processing Activities) and supports principles §7 and §16.
>
> **Sensitivity tiers**:
> - **Standard Personal** — identifiable personal data with no special sensitivity
> - **Sensitive Personal** — data with heightened privacy implications (financial, immigration, behavioural)
> - **Special Category** — Article 9 UK GDPR special category data (health, disability, ethnicity, biometric, etc.)
>
> **Lawful bases (UK GDPR)**:
> - **Contract** — processing necessary for performance of a contract with the data subject
> - **Legal Obligation** — processing necessary for compliance with a legal obligation
> - **Legitimate Interests** — processing necessary for the controller's legitimate interests, not overridden by the individual's rights
> - **Vital Interests** — processing necessary to protect life (emergency use only)
> - **Explicit Consent** — for special category data where no other basis applies
